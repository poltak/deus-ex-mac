# The dance club ending (start with --url=99_Endgame4): a camera above the ceiling shows the dancers.
# The engine does not draw a level surface that the camera sees from behind, and such a surface hides nothing.
# The original has this rule in URender::OccludeBsp of Render.dll. One camera move of this map pushes the camera
# back through the ceiling. Before the fix the ceiling filled the picture, and the picture was black.
# The camera moves of the map come in a random order. So the test puts all camera points at one place above the
# ceiling, with the view down. The next move of the map then puts the camera there, and it stays there.
# tests/render-backface.expect has the lines that the log must have.
1.0  setprop CameraPoint cmd CAMCMD_MOVE
1.0  setprop CameraPoint Location (X=-667,Y=-176,Z=249)
1.0  setprop CameraPoint Rotation (Pitch=52424,Yaw=71552,Roll=0)
4.0  dump DeusExPlayer 0 ViewRotation
4.0  dump ScriptedPawn 999999 bHidden
4.0  visible
4.5  shot 1-above-ceiling
