# Liberty Island (start with --url=01_NYC_UNATCOIsland --tracepath=Terrorist34): the way of a guard on patrol.
# A guard on patrol asks the engine for the next navigation point on its way to a patrol point (FindPathToward in
# the state Patrolling of ScriptedPawn). Terrorist34 goes from PatrolPoint37 to PatrolPoint38 along the reach specs:
# PathNode194, then PathNode864, then PatrolPoint38.
# Before the fix, the engine took the navigation points that the guard could go to in a straight line, 8 at most,
# and sent the guard to the one with the shortest way from there to the goal. At PathNode1005 the test for a
# straight walk to PathNode864, which is 689 units away, gave "no" with 30 frames per second and "yes" with 60,
# because the guard stood 0.08 units away from its place in the other run. With 30 frames per second the guard
# then took the south way through PathNode431, and with 60 the north way.
# The Engine.dll starts the search at the navigation point that the pawn stands on (APawn::findPathToward and
# FSortedPathList::FindVisiblePaths). Then the result comes from the reach specs only, and a small difference of the
# place of the pawn does not change it.
# The engine option --tracepath=Terrorist34 writes one log line for each search of the guard: the place, the goal,
# the point that the guard stands on ("on"), and the next point ("next").
# tools/regress.sh runs this script with 30 and with 60 frames per second. The lines of tests/npc-patrol-path.expect
