Deus Ex (2000, Game of the Year Edition) as a native app for Apple Silicon Macs. The engine is a fork of [SurrealEngine](https://github.com/dpjudas/SurrealEngine), and it plays the files of your own copy of the game.

This download has no game files. You need your own copy of Deus Ex: Game of the Year Edition from Steam or GOG.

## What you need

- A Mac with Apple Silicon and macOS 15 or later
- The game folder of the Windows version of Deus Ex GOTY (version 1112fm)

## Install

1. Download `DeusEx-mac-<version>.zip` below, open it, and move `Deus Ex.app` into your Applications folder.
2. Get the game files and put them at `~/Games/Deus Ex`. The [README](https://github.com/poltak/deus-ex-mac#step-2-get-the-game-files) shows three ways, and one of them uses only Steam on your Mac.
3. Open the app. The app has no signature from an Apple developer account, so macOS stops it at the first start. Click Done, then open System Settings, Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway. The [README](https://github.com/poltak/deus-ex-mac#step-3-open-the-app-for-the-first-time) has the full steps and a Terminal command that does the same.
4. If the app does not find the game folder, it asks you to select it.

The state of the port and the known problems are in the [README](https://github.com/poltak/deus-ex-mac#readme). To report a problem, open an issue.

The license texts of the app and its libraries are in `Deus Ex.app/Contents/Resources/Licenses`.
